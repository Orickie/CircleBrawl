Rival circle

Circles bounce freely and bumping into each other does no damage. Only a class ability hurts: the Knight's swords, the Archer's arrows or the Spike's spikes. Defense is the share of each hit that is blocked. A circle holds up to 3 of its class's traits, each with 3 levels. Tap a tool during a fight to use it. Every win pays 10 coins. After 45 seconds sudden death begins. Lose once and the run ends.
