Circles bounce freely and fight on their own, starting each fight at full HP. Tap a tool during a fight to use it. Every win pays 10 coins. After 40 seconds sudden death begins and both circles take growing damage. A circle holds up to 3 different traits; after that, trait rewards upgrade the ones it has, and a fully upgraded circle gets 25 coins instead. Lose once and the run ends.
